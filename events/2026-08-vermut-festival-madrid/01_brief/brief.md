## Event Basics

- **EVENT NAME:** Vermut Festival Madrid — 4ª Edición
- **EVENT TYPE:** Multi-day urban food + music festival
- **DATE + DURATION:** 22–24 August 2026 (Friday–Sunday, 3 days)
- **VENUE + LOCATION:** Mercado de San Fernando (Lavapiés, Madrid) + Plaza de Lavapiés exterior. 4 outdoor stages distributed in the plaza.

## Venue Character

1940s traditional Madrid neighborhood market (mercado de barrio). Original azulejos (tile work), wrought-iron structural elements, vintage hand-painted stall signage, distinctly castizo working-class atmosphere. The venue is a *living working market repurposed for the festival* — not a polished heritage site.

Two-zone venue:
- **Interior (market):** food, vermut, intimate energy, original tilework + ironwork as ambient texture
- **Exterior (Plaza de Lavapiés):** 4 LED stages, crowd flow, multicultural Lavapiés street energy

This duality — castizo heritage interior wrapped by Lavapiés contemporary multicultural plaza — is the spatial signature of the event.

## Audience

- **AUDIENCE SIZE:** 8,000 attendees expected across the weekend
- **AUDIENCE DEMOGRAPHIC:**
  - Age range: 22–35 (core)
  - Geography: 80% Madrid capital, 20% rest of Spain
  - Profession / context: *gente de barrio + creativos + universitarios* — neighborhood locals + creative class + university students
  - Cultural: castizo Madrid mixed with multicultural Lavapiés
- **AUDIENCE SOPHISTICATION:** **Mainstream-pro** *(inferred — see follow-up #2)*. Strong cultural literacy via TikTok / IG, fluent in current internet aesthetics, but not strictly an industry-insider crowd. Sits between mass and creative-class.
- **AUDIENCE BEHAVIOR:**
  - Posting: heavy. TikTok-native, will post the entire weekend.
  - Existing audience: 80k IG followers, custom hashtag and filters already live.
  - Formality: zero. Casual, colloquial, irreverent.
  - UGC volume: very high — branding must survive being remixed, cropped, filtered by users.

## Context Tension

**YES — multiple layers, this is the most important field for Node 2:**

1. **Heritage venue + Gen-Z TikTok crowd.** 1940s castizo market vs. an audience whose visual reference is Reels and post-internet aesthetics.
2. **Continuity break with prior editions.** Carlos states the previous three editions leaned "vintage bonito" and the brand no longer fits. This year demands discontinuity.
3. **Lavapiés gentrification optics.** The festival operates in a working-class, multicultural neighborhood where "pretty heritage" branding can read as appropriation / sanitization. A rawer, more contemporary treatment may actually be more *respectful* than nostalgic.

→ Project Instructions explicitly flag this as a **Subversion stance** candidate at Node 2. Confirmed by the brief.

## Tone & Visual Direction

- **TONE KEYWORDS:** bold, castizo (raw register, not nostalgic), contemporary, unfiltered, irreverent
- **VISUAL KEYWORDS:** azulejos (treated as texture, not nostalgia), wrought iron, vermut glassware, hand-painted market signage, urban Lavapiés street life
- **COLOR PREFERENCES:** *Decide for me* — with a hard NO on earth tones / saturated mustards
- **TYPOGRAPHY FEEL:** *Decide for me* — with a hard NO on wood-burning vintage typography. Likely vector: contemporary display + sans, with castizo personality (possibly stenciled or hand-painted-feeling) but explicitly NOT artisan-y.
