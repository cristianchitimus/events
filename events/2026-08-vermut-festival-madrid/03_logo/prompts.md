## Wordmark decision

**Canonical wordmark:** `VERMUT FESTIVAL MADRID`

- "VERMUT" is the dominant typographic element (largest, heaviest)
- "FESTIVAL MADRID" supports beneath in same family at smaller scale
- Edition number (`4ª`) does **NOT** belong in the perennial logo — it belongs on event-specific applications (banners, programs, posters)
- Year tag `'26` (apostrophe-26) is permitted as an optional Space Mono numeric anchor in the master lockup, treated as a callout, not a structural element

**Why this configuration:**
- "VERMUT" is the recognition word — drink culture is the festival's soul, "Festival Madrid" is the locator
- Single dominant word survives crops, thumbnails, and small applications
- Per Meaning Map element 5 (oversize numerals as graphic anchor), the `'26` carries the brand's mono-numeral grammar

## Stage 1 — Ideogram (Wordmark Generation)

**Tool:** Ideogram.ai → Generate
**Setting:** Magic Prompt **OFF** (we want exact prompt control)
**Aspect:** 16:9 or 3:2
**Style:** Auto / Design
**Quantity:** generate at least 12 candidates → Carlos picks top 3 → Carlos picks 1 final from top 3

### Prompt (paste directly into Ideogram):

```
A professional wordmark logo for "VERMUT FESTIVAL MADRID", a multi-day urban
food and music festival in Madrid, Spain.

Typography style: brutal chunky display sans-serif in the spirit of Archivo
Black — very heavy weight, tight letter-spacing, blunt geometric letterforms,
deliberately unrefined, internet-loud and contemporary, NOT vintage. The word
"VERMUT" should be the dominant element, set very large; "FESTIVAL MADRID"
appears beneath as supporting text in the same family at roughly one-third
the height. Optional: a small numeric anchor "'26" treated as a Space Mono
mono-spaced callout positioned to the side, not below.

Color: hot scarlet red (#E5261C) letterforms on a warm newsprint off-white
(#F2EEE6) background. No color gradients. Solid flat color only.

Mood: raw, loud, irreverent, immediate, unsanitized.

Format: single horizontal lockup. Clean, scalable, no decorative
illustrations, no taglines beyond the title, no extra text, no icons,
no glassware, no bottles.

AVOID: wood-burning vintage typography, mustard yellow, terracotta, burnt
umber, warm earth tones, hand-drawn folk illustration, abstract female
figures, Etsy illustration aesthetic, tarot illustration, rounded corner UI,
soft gradients, friendly mascot, SaaS visual language, sepia photography,
hand-drawn floral borders, rustic charm, farmer's market poster aesthetic,
chalkboard menu lettering, woodburn lettering, vermut glass icon, wine
bottle illustration, grape clusters, generic startup logos, gradient mesh,
default sans-serif (Helvetica, Arial), lens flares, 3D bevel, drop shadows,
outline strokes, pastel hand-illustrated characters, script fonts,
calligraphy, brush lettering, decorative serifs, ornaments.
```

### Selection criteria (Carlos picking 3 of 12):

✅ **Look for:**
- "VERMUT" reads as the visual anchor — heaviest, biggest, most insistent
- Letterforms feel chunky and confident, not designed-precious
- The wordmark works at thumbnail size (test: shrink to 200px wide — still readable?)
- Strong asymmetry or tension somewhere — the mark shouldn't feel "centered and resolved"
- Color is the right red — not pinkish, not orange-leaning

❌ **Reject:**
- Anything where the typeface looks like default Inter or Helvetica
- Any candidate with iconography (glasses, bottles, flourishes)
- Anything that reads "vintage" or "retro" — even subtly
- Anything with rounded letterform corners
- Anything where "FESTIVAL MADRID" competes with "VERMUT" for hierarchy
- Any candidate that feels gallery-coded / insider / cryptic (we agreed: accessible subversion, not edgy)

## Stage 2 — Nano Banana 2 / Gemini (Variations + Mockups)

**Tool:** Gemini (Nano Banana 2 image model) — gemini.google.com
**Input:** the 1 chosen logo from Stage 1 (uploaded as reference image)

### Prompt (paste into Gemini with logo uploaded):

```
Using the uploaded VERMUT FESTIVAL MADRID logo as the exact reference,
generate the following 6 variations. Maintain logo proportions, letterforms,
kerning, and weight EXACTLY across all variations — only the requested
attribute changes per variation.

1. DARK MODE: Same wordmark, but letterforms in warm off-white (#F2EEE6)
   on a near-black background (#131211). All other elements identical.

2. MONOCHROME WHITE: Pure white (#FFFFFF) version of the wordmark on a
   transparent background. For use on dark photo backgrounds.

3. MONOCHROME BLACK: Pure black (#000000) version on a transparent
   background. For use on light photo backgrounds and print where color
   is unavailable.

4. MOCKUP — WRISTBAND: Logo printed on a paper festival wristband
   (Tyvek-style, 25mm tall, slight wrinkle, attached to a wrist).
   Photographed under harsh on-camera flash, slight motion blur,
   contemporary editorial photography style. Wristband is hot scarlet
   red (#E5261C) with white logo printed on it.

5. MOCKUP — A1 STREET POSTER: Logo featured on an A1-size street poster,
   pasted onto a brick wall in Madrid's Lavapiés neighborhood with visible
   wear (slight rip on corner, tape remnants from previous posters). Shot
   with on-camera flash, harsh shadows, blown highlights on the paper.
   Poster has the logo prominently, white on the scarlet red ground, with
   "22-24 AGOSTO 2026" set in a small Space Mono-style monospace below
   the wordmark.

6. MOCKUP — STEP-AND-REPEAT PHOTOCALL: Logo on a step-and-repeat
   photocall wall at a festival entrance. The wall has a tiled pattern of
   the wordmark plus three sponsor logos (Cinzano, Mahou, Estrella Galicia)
   visible in a row beneath. Shot from a slight angle, harsh flash,
   contemporary festival press-photo style.

AVOID: wood-burning vintage typography, mustard yellow, terracotta, burnt
umber, warm earth tones, hand-drawn folk illustration, abstract female
figures, Etsy illustration aesthetic, tarot illustration, rounded corner UI,
soft gradients, friendly mascot, SaaS visual language, sepia photography,
golden hour soft light, hand-drawn floral borders, rustic charm filter,
farmer's market poster aesthetic, chalkboard menu lettering, vermut glass
icon, wine bottle illustration, grape clusters, default sans-serif, gradient
mesh, lens flares, 3D bevel.
```

### Output expected:
- 1× dark-mode lockup
- 1× white monochrome
- 1× black monochrome
- 3× mockup photos (wristband, A1 poster, photocall)

## Stage 3 — Recraft (Vectorization)

**Tool:** Recraft.ai → Image → Vectorize
**Input:** the chosen primary logo PNG from Stage 1 + the dark/white/black variants from Stage 2

### Steps:
1. Upload `logo-primary.png` to Recraft → Vectorize → download `logo-primary.svg`
2. Upload `logo-monochrome-white.png` → Vectorize → download `logo-monochrome-white.svg`
3. Upload `logo-monochrome-black.png` → Vectorize → download `logo-monochrome-black.svg`
4. Open each SVG in Illustrator or Figma → verify clean paths → manually clean any artifacts
5. Save final SVGs to `03_logo/` in the repo

### Quality check:
- All paths closed (no open strokes)
- No raster fragments inside the SVG
- Letterforms maintain their original geometry — no Bezier softening
- File size <50kb each
