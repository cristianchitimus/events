# Direction B — CRUCE

**Stance:** Adaptive Tension — brand bridges the venue's heritage and the audience's contemporary energy. Sits deliberately between worlds.

**Why this stance for THIS venue + audience:**
*Cruce* (Spanish for "crossing / intersection") refers to the Plaza de Lavapiés as a literal crossroads — and to the festival as a meeting point of generations and cultures. For a mainstream-pro audience this lands as "current but grounded" — neither nostalgic nor edgy. Sponsor-safest of the three. The risk is middle-of-the-road polish: in trying to please both audiences it can fail to claim ground in either.

2. Vermut bottle cross-section illustration (technical, not folkloric)
3. Ticket-stub / admission-tag mark used as a recurring graphic element

## How it handles the venue
Photographs the venue honestly but layouts read contemporary editorial — venue is one ingredient among several.

## How it speaks to the audience

## Honest assessment for the recommendation
This direction is a credible compromise. It would land safely with sponsors and not alienate the existing 80k IG community. But the brief explicitly demands *discontinuity*, and Cruce is calibrated for *continuity-with-an-update*. It under-delivers against Carlos's "más bold" mandate.
