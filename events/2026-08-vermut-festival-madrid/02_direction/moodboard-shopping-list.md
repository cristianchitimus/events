# Moodboard Shopping List — CRUDO

> 10 images to collect. Use Pinterest, Unsplash, Behance, or direct venue photography.
> CRITICAL: slots 1–2 are reference photos of the actual venue. Slots 3–10 are stylistic references.

---

## Slot 1 — Mercado de San Fernando, INTERIOR (mandatory venue ref)

**What to shoot / find:** wide interior shot showing original azulejos + wrought-iron structure + working stalls. NOT a stylized empty editorial shot. The working messy real one — vendors visible, products on display, normal Tuesday energy.

**Source:** Existing photography from Carlos's team if available. Otherwise: Google Images "Mercado de San Fernando Lavapiés" → find an interior shot that shows the lived-in working market character. Avoid the touristy clean-empty hero shots.

## Slot 2 — Plaza de Lavapiés, EXTERIOR (mandatory venue ref)

**What to shoot / find:** daytime exterior crowd shot showing Lavapiés multicultural neighborhood character. Should show: real residents, mixed ages and ethnicities, the actual rhythm of the plaza. Not an empty architecture shot.

**Source:** Carlos's team, or stock photography of "Plaza Lavapiés Madrid daytime crowd". Avoid drone shots and architecture-only frames.

---

## Slot 3 — Harsh flash photography reference

**Search:** Pinterest → `"harsh flash photography street style Madrid"` or `"on-camera flash editorial photography"` or `"Juergen Teller flash photography"` (he's the touchstone)

**Why:** sets the photographic treatment — direct flash, blown highlights, motion, no curation.

## Slot 4 — Brutalist Spanish independent design

**Search:** Pinterest → `"brutalist Spanish editorial poster 2020s"` or `"Folch Studio poster"` or look up Studio Mut, Hey Studio raw work, La Casa de Carlota

**Why:** establishes a contemporary Spanish brutalist visual register that isn't German or Swiss.

## Slot 5 — Oversize numerals as graphic anchor

**Search:** Pinterest → `"big numerals graphic design poster"` or `"swiss design number type poster"` (then strip the swiss restraint)

**Why:** how to make dates / stage IDs / prices into the visual hero rather than the footnote.

## Slot 6 — Post-internet rave / club flyer aesthetic

**Search:** Pinterest → `"post-internet rave flyer red yellow"` or `"club poster 2024 brutalist"` or look up Berlin / Madrid club night flyers from 2023–2025

**Why:** this is the audience's existing visual vocabulary. We're not inventing a language; we're speaking the one they already read.

## Slot 7 — Zine layout with photocopy / print artifact texture

**Search:** Pinterest → `"zine layout photocopy texture"` or `"riso print poster"` or `"CMYK misregistration poster design"`

**Why:** how to make print artifacts into intentional aesthetic choices, not "mistakes."

## Slot 8 — Archivo Black layout examples

**Search:** Pinterest → `"Archivo Black poster layout"` or `"chunky sans serif headline editorial"`

**Why:** confirms the typeface works at scale and shows the layout grammar that complements it.

## Slot 9 — Spanish independent publication / fanzine design

**Search:** Pinterest or Behance → `"Spanish independent magazine design 2020s"` or look up `Pliego`, `Apartamento Magazine`, `Eldiario.es` editorial

**Why:** anchors the brand in real Spanish contemporary culture, not international generic.

## Slot 10 — Madrid Lavapiés street life at night, flash-lit

**Search:** Unsplash / Flickr → `"Madrid Lavapiés street night"` (filter for flash / harsh light)

**Why:** ties together photographic treatment + venue + audience reality. This is what the festival's UGC will actually look like; the brand has to live inside that visual world.

---

## Curation note

When the moodboard is assembled, lay out all 10 images on a single canvas. Test:
1. Does anything in the moodboard belong on the anti-style list? If yes, replace it.
2. Do slots 1–2 (venue) feel coherent with slots 3–10 (style)? If they feel disconnected, the direction is wrong, not the moodboard.
3. Could a stranger guess the brand stance from this moodboard alone? If they say "vintage festival" or "rave flyer" — tune. If they say "raw Madrid market festival, contemporary" — locked.
