# Anti-Style List — CRUDO

> 5 things we will NOT do. Concrete and negatable.
> Auto-injected as `AVOID:` at the end of every image generation prompt at Nodes 3, 4, 5, 8.
> Verbatim client phrasing preserved where given.

---

## 1. Wood-burning vintage typography
**Negate as:** "no woodburn, no rough engraved letters, no carved-into-wood lettering, no rustic burned-edges type, no fake-aged stamped serif, no 'wild west saloon' display fonts, no pyrography aesthetic"

## 2. Earth tones / saturated mustards
**Negate as:** "no mustard yellow, no terracotta, no burnt umber, no warm browns, no sage greens, no dusty earth palette, no autumnal saturation, no 70s coffee-shop palette"

## 3. "Feminist illustrator Etsy" illustration style
**Negate as:** "no hand-drawn folk illustrations of plants and bodies, no abstract female-figure motifs, no botanical line drawings, no flat-color collage illustrations, no pastel-palette hand-illustrated characters, no Etsy-grade decorative illustration, no tarot-deck aesthetics"

## 4. Sanitized / corporate-coded design
**Negate as:** "no rounded corners on layouts, no soft gradients, no friendly mascot, no 'startup deck' aesthetic, no SaaS marketing site visual language, no centered hero with single CTA button, no smooth-blend illustrations, no minimalist white-space-as-luxury treatment"

## 5. "Vintage pretty" venue treatment
**Negate as:** "no sepia photography, no soft golden-hour light, no shallow depth-of-field bokeh, no hand-drawn floral borders or wreath frames, no 'rustic charm' filter, no Lightroom-preset warm-and-faded look, no 'farmer's market poster' aesthetic, no chalkboard menu lettering"
