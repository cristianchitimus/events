# Direction A — SOLERA

**Stance:** Reverence — brand defers to the venue's heritage character.

**Why this stance for THIS venue + audience:**
The 1940s Mercado de San Fernando is genuinely beautiful. A reverent treatment would frame that beauty cleanly and let the venue speak. For a mainstream-pro audience that consumes visual content fast, this reads as "premium / hospitable / safe." The cost is that this is essentially what the previous three editions already attempted, and Carlos has explicitly named it as the trap to escape.
