# Direction C — CRUDO ⭐ RECOMMENDED

**Stance:** Subversion (tactical pivot variant) — venue stays *visible* in photography, but the brand layer on top fights it: brutal typography, internet-loud color, harsh photographic treatment. The market is photographed honestly. The brand refuses to be pretty about it.

**Why this stance for THIS venue + audience:**
"Crudo" = raw, unfiltered, unpolished. Castizo register (un mercado has *producto crudo*) and double-meaning ("una crítica cruda" = harsh, unsparing). For a mainstream-pro TikTok audience, this reads instantly without needing insider references — flash photography, oversize sans, internet-loud red and cyan are vocabulary they already speak. The venue heritage isn't denied; it's *recontextualized* — photographed harshly and overlaid with a brand voice that refuses the museum frame. This handles the Lavapiés gentrification optic by *not* prettifying a working-class market for tourists.

**Variant slot for 4 stages (Appendix A):** the master Crudo system reserves ONE color token (the secondary slot) and ONE numeric mark per stage. Stage 1 = cyan + ❶, Stage 2 = magenta + ❷, etc. Master red and master typography stay constant. Decision on stage-specific palettes happens at Node 3.

## Palette

| Token | Name | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Rojo Crudo | `#E5261C` | Cinzano red recoded as digital scream — internet-loud, NOT earthy |
| Secondary | Cian Pavimento | `#00B5D8` | Aggressive cyan — the "violator color" (no part of venue's natural palette is cyan; that's the deliberate clash). **This is the slot reserved for per-stage variants.** |
| Accent | Amarillo Sirena | `#F2E63C` | Siren yellow — used minimally for callouts, CTAs, ticket prices |
| Light | Periódico | `#F2EEE6` | Newsprint off-white, slightly warm — only "old" element, treated unironically |
| Dark | Negro Mercado | `#131211` | Near-black with slight warmth — never pure `#000` |

**Cinzano alignment:** the primary red is intentionally close to Cinzano's brand red. Master sponsor visibility is built into the palette structure — co-branded layouts will look cohesive without requiring concession.

## Typography
- **Heading:** Archivo Black (Google Fonts) — chunky, brutal, broadly legible at thumbnail size
- **Body:** Inter (Google Fonts) — neutral, fast-reading
- **Display accent:** Space Mono (Google Fonts) — for numerals, timestamps, prices, ironic mono callouts

All free, all on Google Fonts, all WCAG-compliant at standard sizes.

## Mood (5 adjectives)
raw · loud · irreverent · immediate · unsanitized

## Visual Motifs (3 concrete elements)
1. **Blown-out flash photography** of venue interiors — overexposed azulejos, slight motion blur, harsh shadows. The market photographed like it's 2 a.m. and someone's iPhone flash hit it, not like a heritage tourism brochure.
2. **Oversize numerals as graphic anchor** — dates, stage IDs, prices, ticket prices, set times treated as primary visual elements (not secondary information).
3. **Visible print artifacts** — registration crosses, halftone, photocopy noise, slight CMYK misregistration as deliberate texture across all surfaces.

## How it handles the venue
The venue is photographed honestly and harshly — flash, blown highlights, motion — and overlaid with brutal type that refuses to be picturesque.

## How it speaks to the audience
"No estás en un museo, estás en un mercado." This is what your weekend actually looks like, not what the tourism board wants you to think it looks like.

## Why this is the recommendation (3-sentence justification)
The brief mandates discontinuity from "vintage bonito" — Reverence repeats the trap, Adaptive Tension softens the break, only Subversion delivers it. The mainstream-pro audience answer means Subversion has to be *accessible* (read instantly, survive crops, work as TikTok thumbnails) rather than insider — Crudo's vocabulary (flash photo, brutal sans, internet-coded color) is exactly that, while the *tactical pivot* answer keeps the venue visible so we don't lose the cultural specificity that makes the festival actually about Lavapiés. The Cinzano-adjacent primary red and the per-stage variant slot already in the system architecture mean we can scale this through 4 stages and the sponsor stack without breaking it.
